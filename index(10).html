THE INTELLIGENCE BRIEF

AI is moving from tools to teammates.

For years, the default AI interaction was simple: ask a question, receive an answer. The emerging agent model changes the unit of work. You give the system an objective, context, tools and boundaries — then it works through a sequence.

That does not mean handing AI unrestricted control. The smarter business design is to decide where AI can research, recommend, draft or execute, and where a person must approve the next step.

“The advantage won't come from using the most AI. It will come from building the right systems around it.”
PUT THIS TO WORK

Stop giving AI isolated tasks.

Try an agent brief instead. Give AI four things before asking it to work:

01 · OUTCOMEThe result you actually want.
02 · CONTEXTThe facts, constraints and business situation.
03 · AUTHORITYWhat AI may do — and what requires approval.
04 · SUCCESS CHECKHow it should verify the work before finishing.
TRY THIS:

“Your objective is to move this opportunity forward without sounding pushy. Review the conversation and context. Identify what may be preventing a response, recommend the best next action, draft the message, and flag anything that requires my approval before it is sent. Before finishing, check that the recommendation supports the objective.”
